Purple Star Schools

A quick primer for Iowa educators

The Iowa Department of Education awards Purple Star status to schools that commit to supporting military-connected students. Here are the essentials before you dive into the full guidance.

Designation checklist

  • Appoint a staff member to serve as the military liaison for students and families.
  • Maintain a public-facing page with resources for military-connected households.
  • Offer awareness programs and supports tailored to military-connected students.
  • Provide staff training on transitions, deployment, and related challenges.
Visit the state overview Schedule a planning call

How we can help

Need to brief your leadership team or refine your application plan? Our staff can share sample talking points, connect you with Soldier & Family Readiness Support Specialists, and point you to peer schools already designated.

Good to know

  • Recognition lasts three years before renewal.
  • Applications run October–December with announcements in March.
  • Schools that fall short can keep building capacity—state support is available.
  • Questions? Email purple.star@iowa.gov.

Month of the Military Child

Purple Up with your school community

Each April we spotlight Guard-connected youth across Iowa. Use these ideas to recognize students, invite families in, and create consistent moments of celebration.

Three easy ways to join in

  1. 25

    April 25 · Military Family Ball

    Formal dinner, dancing, and surprises for every age group. Reserve your spot before seats run out.

  2. 10

    April 10 · Purple Up Friday

    Wear purple and share a photo with #IApurpleup to shout out Guard-connected youth statewide.

  3. All

    All April · Share support

    Update your social media profile picture, host hallway displays, and invite Guard families to speak in your classrooms.
