Month of the Military Child

Celebrate with us each April

Military families with youth are invited to join the statewide celebration of Month of the Military Child (MOMC). We purple up, gather together, and highlight Guard kids whose strength keeps Iowa mission-ready.

Celebrate with us

  1. 25

    April 25 · Military Family Ball

    Formal dinner, dancing, and surprises for every age group. Reserve your spot before seats run out.

  2. 10

    April 10 · Purple Up Friday

    Wear purple and share a photo with #IApurpleup to shout out Guard-connected youth statewide.

  3. All

    All April · Share support

    Update your social media profile picture, host hallway displays, and invite Guard families to speak in your classrooms.

Where do I pick up the Classroom Kit?

You’ll receive an email or phone call from your local Soldier & Family Readiness Support Specialist. If you don’t hear from them, reach out and we’ll connect you.

Do we have to celebrate on the same day?

We encourage everyone to purple up together, but we know schedules vary. Choose a date that works for your staff, students, or youth group—we’re cheering you on.

How do I explain MOMC?

Month of the Military Child honors kids who serve in their own way when a loved one wears the uniform. Use the classroom kit talking points or invite us to speak at your assembly.
