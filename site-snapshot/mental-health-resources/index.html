Mental Health Resources

Curated support options for Guard-connected youth, caregivers, and service members — organized by type so you can find help fast.

This page is shared privately. If you need immediate help, call or text 988 and press 1 for the Veterans & Military Crisis Line.

Immediate help

Crisis Lines & Hotlines

Available 24/7 — reach out any time, day or night.

Veterans & Military Crisis Line

Immediate Crisis Support · Nationwide

Primary resource for anyone experiencing suicidal ideation or a severe emotional crisis. Available 24/7.

Dial 988, then press 1

Iowa Helpline

Crisis Hotline · Statewide, IA

A 24/7 service providing confidential support and referrals for mental health and substance abuse.

Visit yourlifeiowa.org →

24/7 Crisis Text Line

Crisis Hotline · Nationwide

Free, 24/7 support for those in crisis. Text 741741 from anywhere in the US to connect with a trained Crisis Counselor.

Visit crisistextline.org →

Crisis Text Line (Military)

Crisis Support · Nationwide

Immediate crisis support via text messaging for families in need.

Text 838255

Foundations 2 Crisis

Crisis Hotline · Des Moines, IA

Offers crisis prevention and intervention services, including a crisis hotline and chat services.

Visit foundation2.org →

For service members & families

Military-Specific Support

Confidential services designed for the unique challenges of military life.

Military OneSource

Confidential Life Counseling · Nationwide

Up to 12 free, confidential sessions per issue for stress, relationships, and grief. Sessions are not reported to Command.

Visit militaryonesource.mil →

Director of Psychological Health (DPH)

Military Psychological Support · 54 Joint Force HQ

Assigned to Joint Force Headquarters to coordinate psychological support and provide 24/7/365 access for assessments and referrals.

Military Family Life Consultant (MFLC)

Behavioral Health Support · Military Units/State Programs

Licensed clinical providers assisting with issues faced throughout the deployment cycle — stress management, reintegration, and more.

AACAP Military Family Resource Center

Specialized Mental Health Guidance · Nationwide

Expert guidance for parents on discussing deployment and anxiety with children and finding qualified professionals.
